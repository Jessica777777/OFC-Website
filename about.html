[HQ Files // Behind the Ops]

Every hand is our mission

We're Mondraw — a team that loves the game as much as we love building it.

We want you to open your phone and step straight into a game. No physical cards, no chips, no waiting for everyone to show up — just tap in and the round begins.

OFC: Pineapple Poker is built around OFC at its core, offering Progressive OFC, OFC Joker, and OFC SNG, alongside familiar table games like Texas Hold'em, Omaha/PLO, Blackjack 21, and Rummy. From hand-reading strategy to real-time showdowns, every mode has its own rhythm and challenge, so there's always something new to discover.

Our team comes from mobile apps and game development, and we stay focused on gameplay design, player experience, and social interaction. We want to bring the table you know onto your phone, so players everywhere can always find a seat of their own.

The mission is live. Your move is next.

Fairness is where every round starts
